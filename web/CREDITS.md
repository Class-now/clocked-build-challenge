# Credits

Fonts are self-hosted through Fontsource packages, under the SIL Open Font License 1.1.

- [Fraunces](https://fontsource.org/fonts/fraunces), by Undercase Type: the wordmark and headings
- [IBM Plex Sans](https://fontsource.org/fonts/ibm-plex-sans) and [IBM Plex Mono](https://fontsource.org/fonts/ibm-plex-mono), by IBM: body text, evidence and numbers
- [Big Shoulders Stencil](https://fontsource.org/fonts/big-shoulders-stencil), by Patric King: the rubber stamps

The paper grain and the worn edge of the stamps are inline SVG filters written for this project.
